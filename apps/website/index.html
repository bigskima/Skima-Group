Good to know

Questions about SKIMA?

Here are a few things to help you understand SKIMA and its services.

What is SKIMA?

SKIMA brings together useful services for daily life and work, including energy refills, transport and delivery, bills and shopping.

Are all SKIMA services available now?

What you can order or book depends on your location and what is available nearby. Open a service to see the options for your area.

Can businesses work with SKIMA?

Yes. SKIMA is designed to support participating stations, logistics companies, drivers, independent providers, merchants and other service partners.

Where can I use SKIMA?

What you can book or buy depends on your location. Open the service you need to see what is available nearby.

Is SKIMA Logistics a SKIMA-owned delivery fleet?

No. SKIMA Logistics connects customers with independent logistics companies and service providers. Customers choose a provider for the work they need; that provider carries out the booked service.

Which energy products can I get through SKIMA Refill?

SKIMA Refill focuses on LPG, with other supported energy products such as fuel, diesel and kerosene available where the service is offered in your area.

Do I need a different account for every service?

No. Sign in with your SKIMA account. You can use your SKIMA Wallet for supported payments, and some services may ask for extra details before you get started.
